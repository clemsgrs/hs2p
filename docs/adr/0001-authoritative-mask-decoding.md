# ADR 0001: Use one authoritative backend for mask decoding

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-20

## Context

Tissue and annotation masks are discrete-label rasters. Previously, hs2p could read a TIFF
again with PIL after the configured backend returned invalid labels, and annotation decoding
could retry automatically with OpenSlide. Those hidden recovery paths made a run's effective
decoder depend on the mask contents and format. They also could not distinguish an
intentionally empty mask from a backend that returned a label-valid all-background level.

## Decision

The effective backend selected for the slide is authoritative for tissue and annotation mask
reads. hs2p validates that backend's output but does not retry another backend or use a
format-specific direct reader. A decode error or invalid label set fails with the mask path,
backend, cause, and guidance to select another backend or regenerate the mask.

A valid precomputed tissue mask containing no configured tissue value remains valid. It emits
one warning with the sample ID, mask path, backend, mask level, and tissue value, and increments
the run's `empty_masks` progress and summary count. Empty masks generated by HSV or SAM2 and
valid single-value annotation masks do not produce that warning or count.

## Consequences

- Runs have one reproducible decoder contract instead of content-dependent recovery.
- Empty precomputed masks remain usable while becoming visible during large runs.
- Operators must explicitly choose another backend or regenerate a mask when decoding fails.
- hs2p does not add a TIFF reader, codec, or repair dependency.
